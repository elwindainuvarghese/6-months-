To My Beautiful Girl, Cianna ❤️

Happy 6 months, my love.

I’ve been staring at this message for a while because, honestly, I don't know how to put six months of us into words.

Six months sounds like just a number. But when I think about it properly… it's six months of waking up and having someone I genuinely want to talk to. Six months of random conversations that somehow become the best part of my day.

Six months of stupid jokes, teasing, little arguments, misunderstandings, laughter, late conversations, random thoughts, and those tiny moments that probably meant nothing to the rest of the world but somehow became memories that I keep close to my heart.

And somewhere between all of those little moments…

you became home to me. ❤️

♡♡♡

I don't even know exactly when it happened. There wasn't one particular day where I suddenly thought, “Okay, she's incredibly important to me now.”

It happened quietly. A little more every day. A conversation here. A laugh there. A random message. A “did you eat?” A “go sleep.” A little teasing. A little caring. A stupid argument followed by us talking again.

And then one day I realized that whenever something happened in my life, whether it was something exciting, something stupid, something funny, or something that hurt…

you were one of the first people I wanted to tell.

That's when I knew. You weren't just someone I loved talking to. You had become a part of my everyday life.

🥺

And I don't think you realize how much your little things mean to me.

The way you randomly check on me. The way you tell me to sleep when I clearly need it. The way you scold me for looking at stars when I should be sleeping. 😭 The way you call me rotudu. The way you somehow manage to care about me while simultaneously making fun of me. The way you can make me smile without even trying.

Sometimes you probably think you're just sending me another normal message. But I swear, there are times when I read something from you and just sit there smiling at my phone like an idiot.

And maybe you haven't seen that smile yet. Maybe you still think I'm nonchalant. 😂 But I wish you could see yourself through my eyes for just one moment. Maybe then you'd understand.

You'd understand why your messages affect me the way they do. Why your little acts of care stay in my head. Why I remember random things you've told me. Why your happiness matters to me. Why when something is wrong with you, I can't just casually pretend I don't care.

Because somewhere along the way… your feelings started mattering to me almost as much as my own. And that is something I don't take lightly.

✨

I also love the ridiculous little world we've created together. Our random future plans. Our impossible conversations that somehow turn into us planning an entire life in ten minutes. 😭❤️

One minute we're talking about something completely normal… and suddenly we're talking about seeing the Northern Lights together. Watching Taare Zameen Par. Having that little beach wedding we've somehow already imagined. Running away to the mountains afterwards.

And honestly? I don't know which of those things we'll actually end up doing. Maybe we'll do all of them. Maybe we'll change our minds about half of them. Maybe life will take us somewhere neither of us has imagined yet.

But that's not even what makes those conversations special to me. It's the person standing beside me in those imaginary pictures. It's you. That's what makes them beautiful. Because whenever I imagine some future version of my life, somehow you're there.

And maybe that's one of the scariest and most beautiful things about loving someone. You don't just start thinking about them in your present. You start accidentally putting them into your future too.

♡♡♡

And I love discovering all the little versions of you. Even the tiny, chaotic child you once were. 😭❤️

I still love hearing your childhood stories. Especially that ridiculous story about the aunty giving you fruits and telling you there was only one floor in her house, and little you basically decided, “Nope, I don't want this,” and gave them back. 😂

I wasn't there. I didn't know that little girl. But somehow, when you tell me these stories, I can picture her. And I love that. Because loving someone isn't only about knowing who they are today. It's also about slowly discovering the person they were before you ever came into their life. The little girl. The teenager. The person who had her own fears, dreams, habits, silly moments and memories.

Every little story you tell me feels like you're giving me another tiny piece of yourself. And I treasure those pieces.

😂

And yes… I haven't forgotten the Kim Taehyung (V) obsession. 🙄😂 And let's not even talk about how super jealous I became of your bodyguard who is in Pune now. 😭

But even that has become one of those stupid little things that makes us feel like us. Because I don't want a love where we have to be serious every second. I want the stupidness too. The teasing. The jealousy jokes. The “arey.” The random Marathi. The nonsense. The laughing for absolutely no reason. The conversations that start with one topic and somehow end somewhere completely unrelated.

I want all of that. Because when I think about us, I don't just think about the big romantic moments. I think about the ordinary ones. And honestly… those are the ones I love the most.

♡♡♡

There is something else I want you to know. I don't love you only because of how you make me feel. I love you for who you are.

I love the way you care. I love your heart. I love the way you can be playful one second and incredibly caring the next. I love how much thought you put into little things. I love the way you remember things. I love the way you can be stubborn. I love the way you tease me. I love your ridiculous side. I love your soft side. I love the version of you that doesn't have everything figured out.

I love the version of you that gets tired. The version that overthinks. The version that needs reassurance. The version that laughs until she can't breathe. The version that gets annoyed with me. The version that randomly sends me something because she thought of me.

I don't need some perfect version of you. I just want you. The real you. The girl behind all the little things everyone else sees.

🌸

And if I'm being completely honest… these six months have changed something inside me. Before you, I think I understood love mostly as a feeling. Now I understand it a little differently.

Love is remembering the little things. Love is wanting to know how someone's day went. Love is caring whether they slept enough. Love is listening to the same story even if you've heard it before. Love is wanting to be there when they're happy. But also wanting to stay when they're not.

Love is being able to laugh together. But also being able to sit through the difficult moments. Love is not expecting every day to be perfect. It's knowing that even when a day isn't perfect… you still want that person in it.

And that's how I feel about you. I don't need every day with you to be some huge romantic movie. Give me the ordinary days. Give me the sleepy conversations. Give me the random calls. Give me the “what are you doing?” Give me the arguments that end in us laughing. Give me the days where nothing special happens.

Because if you're there… they'll still be special to me.

♾️

And Cianna… I don't know what the future has planned for us. I genuinely don't. Life can change so much. People grow. Plans change. Dreams change. Circumstances change.

But there is something I can promise you about the way I feel right now. I don't want to stop choosing you.

I want to keep learning you. Keep understanding you. Keep discovering the little things you don't even realize about yourself. I want to keep hearing your stories. I want to keep making you laugh. I want to keep being the idiot you call rotudu. I want to keep annoying you. I want to keep learning Marathi just because it makes you smile. I want to keep hearing you say I'm nonchalant while secretly hoping you'll eventually admit I'm not. 😭❤️

I want to keep making those ridiculous future plans. And maybe one day… we'll look back at the things we used to imagine and laugh because we actually did some of them.

Maybe we'll really stand under the Northern Lights. Maybe we'll actually watch Taare Zameen Par together. Maybe we'll really have that beach wedding. Maybe we'll disappear into the mountains for a while. Or maybe our future will look completely different.

And honestly? I'm okay with not knowing exactly what it looks like.

Because the most beautiful part is that right now… I get to imagine it with you.

❤️

Six months ago, I didn't know how important you would become to me. I didn't know your name would become one of my favourite notifications. I didn't know your messages would become something I'd look forward to. I didn't know I'd start remembering the smallest things you say.

I didn't know I'd smile at my phone because of you. I didn't know I'd care this deeply about how your day went. I didn't know I'd have someone I could call my person.

And I definitely didn't know that six months later… I'd be sitting here trying to find words big enough to explain what you mean to me.

But maybe that's the problem. There aren't words big enough. Because how do I explain what it feels like when one person slowly becomes part of your everyday life? How do I explain the comfort of knowing they're there? How do I explain missing someone even when you've only just stopped talking?

I don't know how. So maybe I don't need to explain it perfectly. Maybe I just need to tell you the simplest truth I know:

I love having you in my life. And I love you. More deeply than I knew I could.

♡♡♡

So, happy six months, my love. ❤️

Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every stupid joke, and every random story. Thank you for every time you checked on me, told me to sleep, and made me smile without knowing. Thank you for trusting me with pieces of yourself. Thank you for letting me know the little girl you used to be, the girl you are now, and all the dreams you carry for the person you're becoming. And thank you for choosing me too.

I don't know where life will take us. But I know that these six months will always be something I'll be grateful for. Because they gave me memories I'll always carry. They gave me moments I'll always smile about. And most importantly… they gave me you.

If I could go back to the beginning and live these six months all over again, knowing every laugh, every argument, every late conversation, every stupid moment and every beautiful one… I'd choose you all over again. Without hesitation. Every single time.

Because out of all the people I could have met… somehow, I got to meet you. And out of all the people I could have fallen for… somehow, my heart found you.

And if I get the privilege of continuing this story with you… I don't want it to just be six months. I want to look back one day and realize that this was only the beginning of a very, very long story. A story filled with ordinary mornings, ridiculous plans, countless memories, stupid arguments, uncontrollable laughter, difficult days we got through together, places we haven't seen yet, dreams we haven't imagined yet… and two people who kept finding their way back to each other.

So here's to six months, baby.

To the girl who became my comfort.
To the girl who became my happiness.
To the girl whose name can still make me smile for no reason.
To the girl who somehow made an ordinary part of my life feel extraordinary.
To my favourite person.

And maybe one day, years from now, when we're sitting somewhere together and remembering how all of this started… I hope I can look at you and say:

“Remember when six months felt like such a big deal?”

And you'll smile. And I'll probably still be the same idiot who can't stop smiling when you look at me. Because some things, I hope, never change. Especially the way my heart feels when it's you. ❤️

Happy six months, my beautiful girl. I love you. Not just for the big moments. Not just for the beautiful days. But for every little ordinary moment in between.

And if I had to choose one person to share all those ordinary moments with… I'd choose you. Today. Tomorrow. And every day that life gives us after this.

Always your idiot,
Elwin. ❤️
